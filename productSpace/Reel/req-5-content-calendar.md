# Req 5: Content Calendar & Slot Suggestions

## Summary
Interactive schedule management for generated clips with smart slot placement and voice scheduling.

## Features
- Month, week, and list views with platform badge colors.
- Drag-and-drop scheduling powered by `@dnd-kit`.
- AI Slot suggestions calculated in **IST** based on [[req-6-performance-loop]] history.
- Voice-driven scheduling ("put the pricing clip on Thursday morning on LinkedIn").
- "Plan My Week" auto-fill optimization across platforms with cadence guardrails.
- `.ics` calendar export.
